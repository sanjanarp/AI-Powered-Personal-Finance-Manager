# Spending Playbooks — What To Do If...

## 🚨 You're Overspending This Month
1. Pause all non-essential spending immediately (dining out, subscriptions, shopping)
2. Review your top 3 spending categories — identify the biggest increases
3. Look for recurring charges you forgot about (subscriptions, memberships)
4. Set a hard daily spending limit for the rest of the month
5. Use cash or a prepaid card to force awareness
6. Cook at home for the remainder of the month to save on food costs

## 🚨 You Missed a Bill Payment
1. Pay it immediately — the sooner you pay, the less damage to your credit
2. Contact the creditor — ask if they can waive the late fee (first-time courtesy)
3. Set up autopay for at least the minimum on all bills going forward
4. Check your credit report in 30 days — payments less than 30 days late usually aren't reported
5. Add the due date to your calendar with a 3-day early reminder

## 🚨 Subscription Creep (Too Many Subscriptions)
1. List every active subscription (check bank statements for the last 3 months)
2. Categorize each as: Essential / Nice-to-Have / Unused
3. Cancel all "Unused" immediately
4. Evaluate "Nice-to-Have" — can you share or find a free alternative?
5. Set a monthly subscription budget cap (e.g., $50/month total)
6. Review subscriptions quarterly

## 🚨 Your Spending Increased Compared to Last Month
1. Compare category-by-category: which areas grew the most?
2. Check for one-time expenses vs. ongoing increases
3. If it's a lifestyle change (new hobby, eating out more), decide if it's sustainable
4. Adjust your budget categories to reflect new priorities
5. If the increase is unintentional, identify and cut the top 2 non-essential categories

## 🚨 You're Living Paycheck to Paycheck
1. Track every expense for 30 days — awareness is step one
2. Cut discretionary spending to the minimum
3. Look for ways to earn extra income (side gig, selling unused items)
4. Build a $500 mini emergency buffer first
5. Automate even $10/week into savings
6. Avoid new debt — no new credit card purchases if you carry a balance
7. Consider the 50/30/20 rule but start with 60/20/20 if needed
