# Budgeting Rules & Methods

## The 50/30/20 Rule
A simple framework for dividing your after-tax income:
- **50% Needs**: Rent, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments
- **30% Wants**: Dining out, entertainment, subscriptions, shopping, hobbies
- **20% Savings & Debt**: Emergency fund, retirement contributions, extra debt payments

If your "needs" exceed 50%, look for ways to reduce fixed costs (e.g., cheaper housing, refinancing).

## Envelope Method
Allocate cash (or virtual envelopes) to each spending category at the start of the month. When an envelope is empty, stop spending in that category. Works best for variable expenses like dining, entertainment, and groceries.

## Zero-Based Budgeting
Every dollar of income is assigned a job: bills, savings, debt, or spending. At the end of the month, income minus all allocations should equal zero. This forces intentional spending and eliminates "leftover" money that disappears.

## Tips for Sticking to a Budget
- Review your budget weekly, not just monthly
- Use the first week of the month to set limits; use the last week to review
- Build in a small "fun money" buffer so you don't feel deprived
- Automate savings transfers on payday
- Track every purchase for the first 3 months to build awareness
