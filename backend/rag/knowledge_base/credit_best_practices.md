# Credit Score Best Practices

## Key Factors Affecting Your Credit Score
1. **Payment History (35%)**: Pay every bill on time. Even one 30-day late payment can drop your score significantly.
2. **Credit Utilization (30%)**: Keep your total credit card balances below 30% of your total credit limit. Below 10% is ideal.
3. **Length of Credit History (15%)**: Keep old accounts open, even if unused. The average age of your accounts matters.
4. **Credit Mix (10%)**: Having a mix of credit types (cards, loans, mortgage) can help, but don't open accounts just for this.
5. **New Credit Inquiries (10%)**: Each hard inquiry can temporarily lower your score by 5-10 points. Space out applications.

## Credit Utilization Tips
- Pay your balance before the statement closing date, not just the due date
- Request credit limit increases (without a hard pull) to lower your utilization ratio
- Spread purchases across multiple cards rather than maxing one out
- Set up autopay for at least the minimum payment on every card

## Building Credit
- If you're starting out, consider a secured credit card
- Become an authorized user on a family member's card with good history
- Use credit for regular purchases you'd make anyway (gas, groceries) and pay in full monthly
- Report rent payments to credit bureaus using services like Experian Boost

## Warning Signs
- Carrying balances that grow month over month
- Only making minimum payments
- Using cash advances (high fees and immediate interest)
- Closing old credit cards (reduces available credit and average account age)
