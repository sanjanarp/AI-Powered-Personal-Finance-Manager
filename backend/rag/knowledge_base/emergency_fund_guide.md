# Emergency Fund Guidelines

## What Is an Emergency Fund?
An emergency fund is money set aside specifically for unexpected expenses: job loss, medical bills, car repairs, or urgent home maintenance. It is NOT for vacations, planned purchases, or regular bills.

## How Much Should You Save?
- **Starter goal**: $1,000 as a mini emergency fund
- **Standard goal**: 3-6 months of essential expenses (rent, food, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments)
- **Conservative goal**: 6-12 months if you're self-employed, have variable income, or are the sole provider

## Where to Keep It
- High-yield savings account (HYSA) — earns interest, still accessible
- Money market account — similar to HYSA with slightly higher rates
- Do NOT invest it in stocks or lock it in CDs — you need liquidity

## How to Build It
1. Calculate your monthly essential expenses
2. Set a target (start with 3 months)
3. Automate a weekly or biweekly transfer to your emergency fund
4. Direct windfalls (tax refunds, bonuses) into the fund
5. Once funded, redirect that automatic transfer to investments or debt

## When to Use It
- Job loss or significant income reduction
- Unexpected medical or dental expenses not covered by insurance
- Essential car or home repairs
- Emergency travel (family emergency)

## When NOT to Use It
- Sales or shopping deals
- Planned expenses (holidays, vacations)
- Non-urgent home improvements
- Upgrading electronics or appliances
